## 1. Product Vision and Principles

### 1.1 Identity [C]

- **Name:** ATHAR – أثر

- **Main slogan:** من أثرٍ جئنا، وإلى أثرٍ نمضي.

- **Supporting message:** ابدأ بخطوات اللي سبقوك، وسيب خطوات يستفيد منها اللي بعدك.

### 1.2 Vision [C]

ATHAR is an Arabic-first, Egypt-first platform that helps people discover digital work platforms, evaluate them through trustworthy information and real experiences, track their own journey, and contribute knowledge that helps others. Its primary motivation is social impact and leaving a positive legacy, not monetization.

### 1.3 What ATHAR is and is not [C]

| ATHAR is                                                     | ATHAR is not                                 |
|--------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| A platform discovery and information resource                | A job marketplace                            |
| A trust and verification system                              | A hiring platform                            |
| A personal journey-tracking tool                             | A traditional social network                 |
| A community-based knowledge-sharing environment (group chat) | A feed, a follower graph, or a messaging app |

### 1.4 Audience [C]

People interested in digital work in Egypt: trainees of initiatives such as DEPI and Digital Egypt Pioneers, beginners and professionals in freelancing, remote work, and AI-model training, and anyone seeking trustworthy digital platforms and opportunities. Naming these initiatives describes an audience only. **No partnership, endorsement, or official relationship with any initiative is claimed or implied without explicit authorization.**

### 1.5 Core value loop (descriptive summary, not a new decision)

Discover a platform, evaluate it through verified facts and real experiences, act and track personal progress, contribute reviews and corrections, and thereby leave a trace for the next person.

### 1.6 Principles [C]

1.  The core experience is free.

2.  No paid ranking, paid placement, or selling of user data.

3.  Trust, transparency, and knowledge sharing come first.

4.  Evidence over opinion: a platform is never labeled fraudulent on a negative review alone.

5.  Arabic-first with full RTL support.

6.  No unnecessary social-network mechanics.

7.  Privacy by default: profiles private, earnings and application details always private.

8.  Knowledge persists in verified facts and reviews, not in feeds.

**EF-01 (editorial flag; wording not changed).** The approved Community copy mentions “أسئلة، إجابات” (questions, answers). In V1 these happen inside members-only group chats. ATHAR has no Q&A feed, so no community screen may show a public question-and-answer list or imply one. The copy is flagged for a future editorial review to confirm it does not promise a public discussion feed.

**RD-I6 Tone-of-voice rule [R, V1].** Warm Egyptian colloquial for invitations, encouragement, and empty states, consistent with the approved copy. Plain, neutral Modern Standard Arabic for legal text, security messages, verification statuses, moderation notices, and precise errors. No colloquial humor in warnings or sanctions. The slogan stays in the approved Modern Standard form. *Why:* resolves the register mix noted in the review. Copy for states other than the zero-result search is not yet approved and will be drafted in this tone during design.

## 7. Content, Verification, and Moderation Policies

### 7.1 Verification workflow

1.  A fact item is entered with its source (or imported from an accepted suggestion).

2.  A reviewer other than the author checks it against sources that meet RD-B6 and sets status, verification date, and next-review due date.

3.  Items publish with their status. Unverifiable items publish as Unconfirmed or stay unpublished, never as Verified.

4.  Due dates follow RD-B4. When an interval ends the item becomes Needs Review automatically.

5.  **Overdue escalation:** at the due date the item shows Needs Review; 14 days later it moves to the top of the Content Admin queue; 30 days after a missed Class 1 date the platform loses Featured eligibility and shows a freshness notice. Nothing is silently removed.

### 7.2 Edit suggestions and new-platform submissions

- **Edit suggestion:** the member picks the fact item, proposes a value, and adds evidence if the change is substantive. A Content Admin reviews it. Decisions target a median of 7 days.

- **Outcomes:** Accepted (the fact is updated and an edit-history entry is created; status becomes Verified only if the evidence meets RD-B6, otherwise Unconfirmed); Rejected (always with a reason such as insufficient evidence, duplicate, or inaccurate); the author is notified either way. Pending may carry a “more information requested” note.

- **New platform:** the member submits a name or website plus basic information. An admin independently checks identity (working website, accessible terms), payment and withdrawal information, Egypt availability, and any warnings. If identity is confirmed but availability is not, the platform may publish with availability Not yet confirmed and Unconfirmed items. If identity cannot be confirmed, it stays unpublished and the member is told why. Publication creates the community automatically.

- **Credit:** accepted contributions appear in the author’s My Contributions and, if their profile is public, as an accepted-contribution count. The public edit history shows what changed and when, not who suggested it.

### 7.3 Platform states

Availability states: Available, Available with restrictions, Unavailable, Suspended or closed, Not yet confirmed. The Warning / under review flag is independent (RD-A2). State changes are dated in a public status history that summarizes the change and the source. Platforms are never deleted for a status change (CD-111). A platform may be merged into another only for duplicates, by a Super Admin, preserving history and the community.

### 7.4 Warning and suspected-scam procedure

1.  **Intake:** at least three independent reports describing the same issue within 30 days, or any single report with documentary evidence, or an official notice. [F]

2.  **Triage:** a Content Admin reviews within 3 days, checks evidence, and tries to confirm with the platform where possible.

3.  **Decision:** open a Warning with a neutral banner and dated note (second approver required). Members tracking the platform are notified.

4.  **Review cycle:** re-reviewed every 14 days; a Warning must be resolved within 60 days by lifting it, replacing it with a sourced factual note (for example, delayed withdrawals since a date, per documented reports), or moving the platform to Suspended or closed.

5.  **Record:** every step is in the audit log and the outcome in the public status history.

**Never:** label a platform fraudulent or a scam on the basis of negative reviews or ratings alone, or publish unevidenced allegations. Strong evidence is published as factual statements with sources. Use of the words “fraud” or “scam” requires second-person approval and legal review [L]. Platforms can send corrections or takedown requests through Contact; these are handled on evidence by a Content Admin [L].

### 7.5 Review policy

Allowed: factual descriptions of experience, including negative ones. Not allowed: the items in RD-C6. Flow: report, Content Admin queue, decision (keep, request edit, hold, remove), notice to the author with a reason code, appeal available. Reviews never alter fact verification status.

### 7.6 Chat content policy

Not allowed: spam or promotion; referral or affiliate links; requests for money, passwords, codes, or personal information; attempts to move people to other apps; harassment, hate, or threats; impersonation; confidential platform test or task content; personal data of others; illegal or sexual content. Enforcement follows RD-D9.

### 7.7 Sanction guide

| Severity | Examples                                     | Typical action                                                           |
|----------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Low      | Off-topic spam, first minor rule break       | Remove content, warn                                                     |
| Medium   | Repeated spam, referral links, harassment    | Temporary mute or restriction                                            |
| High     | Scam attempts, doxxing, threats, ban evasion | Immediate restriction pending review; suspension or ban by a Super Admin |

### 7.8 Transparency

The Community & Content Guidelines and How Verification Works pages are public. Every moderation notice states the reason and the appeal path. A periodic public transparency summary is a Later option.

## 13. Content Seeding and Launch Readiness

### 13.1 Seeding strategy

- **Directory:** launch with about 30 to 40 platforms fully verified (all core facts, sources, dates) across at least six categories. Add further platforms only with verified identity and clearly labeled Unconfirmed items if capacity allows. Choose by relevance to Egyptians, documentation quality, category breadth, and demand heard from pilot members. Initial verification is estimated at 2 to 3 reviewer-hours per platform [F]. No unsourced facts.

- **Reviews:** no fabricated or staff-written reviews. Pilot members who genuinely used a platform are invited to use the standard review form. No payment for reviews; any incentive must be rating-neutral and disclosed [L].

- **Communities:** all platform communities are created automatically. The General community is moderated daily during the first 30 days. Each community starts with the pinned Start here item and rules. The pilot cohort is concentrated on the roughly ten most relevant platform communities. No bots or simulated activity.

- **Help Center:** at least 20 FAQ entries at launch (signup, statuses, ratings, privacy, reporting, deletion, export).

- **Partnerships:** none are claimed (RD-J5).

### 13.2 Launch readiness gates (minimum)

| Area              | Required before public launch                                                                                                                                                                           |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Moderation        | At least 3 trained moderators, or 2 plus a Super Admin, with daily coverage; moderation handbook and sanction guide; queues and appeals tested; blocklist configured; named owners for response targets |
| Verification      | Written procedure; at least 2 trained reviewers; every core fact of launch platforms verified in the 30 days before launch; no overdue Class 1 item; second-approver rule workable                      |
| Support           | Contact channel with a 2-business-day target and a named owner; Help Center live; data export and deletion tested end to end; takedown and legal-notice route defined                                   |
| Legal and privacy | Privacy Policy and Terms reviewed by counsel; data-protection obligations assessed; Guidelines published; retention schedule approved; privacy contact named; incident-response plan                    |
| Security          | Admin two-factor enforced; audit log operating; independent security review done; abuse limits active                                                                                                   |
| Quality           | Arabic RTL checks on mobile, tablet, desktop; WCAG 2.1 AA check; real-time chat reliability test; light and dark modes verified                                                                         |
| Governance        | At least two Super Admins; conflict-of-interest declarations on file                                                                                                                                    |

### 13.3 Staged release

**Closed pilot:** invite-only, roughly 100 to 300 members for 4 to 6 weeks [F], measuring M-04 to M-11. **Public launch:** when all gates are met, response targets held during the pilot, and no unresolved high-severity trust or privacy issue remains.

## Closing Notes

### Summary of important changes from v1.0

1.  The homepage community section no longer shows discussions; it shows aggregate Active Communities (CH-01).

2.  Notification triggers are precise, and “interactions on contributions” is defined without social reactions (CH-02).

3.  Communities are members-only group chats with automatic platform communities, link and scam controls, reporting, sanctions, appeals, pins, and in-community search.

7.  My ATHAR clarifies Favorites, Platforms, Applications, and Earnings, adds application states, and sets locked, disclosed currency conversion.

8.  Accounts gain a minimum age, deletion and export flows, a retention schedule, security defaults, and a full admin permission matrix with an audit log.

9.  The document adds supporting pages, states, success metrics, seeding, launch gates, and an open-questions list for legal and external validation.

10. All approved brand copy is preserved exactly.
